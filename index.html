🔷 Shape Surface Area — minimize material

Enter volume (cm³) and choose a shape. The calculator gives optimal dimensions (approx) and surface area.

🌾 Agriculture — minimize fertilizer cost

Simple model: base yield per hectare + yield increase per kg fertilizer. Compute fertilizer needed to reach a target total yield.

💹 Economics — maximize profit (simple demand)

Demand: P = a − bQ. Cost: C = cQ + F. Profit max occurs at Q* = (a − c) / (2b) (if positive).
